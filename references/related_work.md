# Related work and attribution

[Scientific overview](../science/overview.md) · [Structured bibliography](references.bib)

The contribution concerns the time needed to erase a no-passing coherent chain through one controlled endpoint. Its constituent ideas have established precedents. This account separates those ingredients from the complete control comparison and filling criterion proved in the scientific pages. The comparison concerns the listed primary sources and versions; it is not an exhaustive guarantee of priority.

## Fragmentation and boundary memory

**Rakovszky, Sala, Verresen, Knap, and Pollmann, Statistical localization: from strong fragmentation to strong edge modes, Physical Review B 101, 125126 (2020).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.101.125126) · [arXiv:1910.06341v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.06341v2). Ordered-spin integrals and persistent edge memory are established in this work, especially Section II.C.2. The rightmost-spin observable used here belongs to that physical setting. The new control statements are not a claim to have discovered ordered-spin memory.

**Wang, Balasubramanian, Han, Lake, Chen, and Yang, Exponentially slow thermalization in 1D fragmented dynamics, arXiv:2501.13930v1 (2025).** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13930v1). Sections II.2 and IV.2, the discussion in VII.0.3, and Appendix E are particularly relevant. They study boundary-induced thermalization of fragmented chains, including the constrained spinful setting, and establish exponential lower bounds in stochastic or random-unitary descriptions. Appendix E uses an unbiased boundary reset. The present [U2](../science/control_obstruction.md) is an explicit physical-time bound for every unital endpoint system protocol under a fixed coherent Hamiltonian. [F1–F2](../science/reservoir_threshold.md) additionally give matching coherent-channel filling dependence and a time-dependent reservoir classification.

**Han, Chen, and Lake, Exponentially slow thermalization and the robustness of Hilbert space fragmentation, arXiv:2401.11294v2 (2026 revision; first submitted 2024).** [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11294v2). This work already gives numerical evidence for slow coherent Hamiltonian boundary relaxation in a pair-flip model and an exponential theorem for the associated random-unitary dynamics. Slow coherent relaxation in a fragmented system is therefore not, by itself, the contribution claimed here.

## Quantum bottlenecks

**Rakovszky, Placke, Breuckmann, and Khemani, Bottlenecks in quantum channels and finite temperature phases of matter, arXiv:2412.09598v1 (2024).** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.09598v1). Equation (4), Theorem 1, and Lemma 2 establish the general bottleneck framework, including sequences of channels with a common stationary state. The [endpoint obstruction](../science/control_obstruction.md) specializes this reasoning to a rank-two current, a fixed product input, a local population witness, and physical elapsed time. It does not introduce a new general bottleneck theorem or the possibility of time-dependent unital comparison channels.

## Local relaxation and reservoir detours

**Burgarth and Giovannetti, Full Control by Locally Induced Relaxation, Physical Review Letters 99, 100501 (2007).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.99.100501) · [arXiv:0704.3027v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.3027v1). Their local-control scheme transfers arbitrary unknown states to and from a larger system using a controlled memory. The expanded account, **A Protocol For Cooling and Controlling Composite Systems by Local Interactions**, [arXiv:0710.0302v4, 14 November 2008](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.0302v4), explains the repeated local cooling and subsequent control construction. Input-independent local preparation is established. The present [E1](../science/erasure_protocol.md) supplies a uniform polynomial-time reservoir-only bound for this chain, together with its unital-control comparator and filling threshold.

**Gal and Raz, Precooling Strategy Allows Exponentially Faster Heating, Physical Review Letters 124, 060602 (2020).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.060602) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.06113). A cold-to-hot switch can remove a slow relaxation component for a specified initial thermal state. Thus using an intermediate reservoir to accelerate approach to a final state is established. Here the comparison is exponential versus polynomial in chain length, with a uniform quantum-channel target and a lower bound against an entire stated control class. Particle removal in the present Hamiltonian is not automatically ground-state cooling.

**Beer and Burgarth, Global zero-excitation state preparation through subsystem cooling, arXiv:2609.23960v1 (21 September 2026).** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23960v1). This close local-loss precedent provides structural conditions for a unique globally attractive zero-excitation state and a chain-length estimate from the one-excitation sector. Its discussion leaves the relationship to the full Liouvillian gap unresolved. The local sink and cubic single-particle scale are established ingredients. The present [charge estimate](../science/charge_transport.md) and [complete erasure proof](../science/erasure_protocol.md) control every physical input and reference with explicit finite-time bounds.

## Classical single-file renewal

**Jansen, Nedea, and Lukkien, Analytical results for the reactivity of a single-file system, Physical Review E 67, 036104 (2003).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.67.036104) · [Preprint: Exact Results for the Reactivity of a Single-File System](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0207358). The classical model has no passing, adsorption and desorption at one end, and a fast-diffusion limit. Equations (2)–(10) develop first-passage renewal and the binomial stationary occupancy; the later analysis uses detailed-balance time reversal. Empty-chain recurrence and the factor $`(1-p)^L`$ therefore have direct classical precedents. The [vacuum effect](../science/vacuum_accessibility.md) and [complete channel lemma](../science/channel_mixing.md) supply the additional coherent-quantum statement needed for F1–F2. The inspected classical work addresses catalytic reactivity, not this full-channel erasure-control classification.

## Charge methods and boundary loss

**Penc, Moca, Legeza, Prosen, Zaránd, and Werner, Loss-Induced Quantum Information Jet in an Infinite Temperature Hubbard Chain, Physical Review Letters 133, 190403 (2024).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.190403) · [arXiv:2402.19390v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.19390v2). Boundary particle loss, spinful transport, and information fronts are established in the Hubbard setting. Its local state space includes double occupancy and its dynamical question differs from uniform finite-chain erasure. The present result uses the exact no-passing qutrit reduction and a subsequent refill stage.

**Zhan, Ding, Huhn, Gray, Preskill, Chan, and Lin, Rapid quantum ground state preparation via dissipative dynamics, Physical Review X 16, 011004 (2026).** [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/wzb3-dbg9) · [arXiv:2503.15827v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.15827v3). Uniform quasi-free preparation and polynomial examples with boundary dissipation are established. The spectral bounds account for eigenvector conditioning. Here the exact Lyapunov Gramian is a self-contained way to obtain a uniform integrated survival estimate for the strictly local charge generator. Its role is technical support for the coherent control comparison, rather than a claim to introduce quasi-free dissipative preparation.

## Complete contraction and channel resources

**Hirche, Quantum Doeblin coefficients: A simple upper bound on contraction coefficients, arXiv:2405.00105v2 (2024)**, [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00105v2), and **George, Hirche, Nuradha, and Wilde, Quantum Doeblin Coefficients: Interpretations and Applications, arXiv:2503.22823v3 (2026 revision)**, [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22823v3), develop quantum replacement-channel minorization, contraction coefficients, and mixing or decoupling applications. [M1](../science/channel_mixing.md) uses these established ideas with a finite charge decomposition and complete Kadison–Schwarz contraction. It provides the explicit argument and constants required by this model, rather than introducing quantum Doeblin theory.

**Liu and Winter, Resource theories of quantum channels and the universal role of resource erasure, arXiv:1904.04201v1 (2019).** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.04201v1). Example 2 and Sections V.2.1–V.2.3 supply the channel-resource framework, complete increasing powers, CP-order max-relative entropy, and robustness. The [resource section](../science/resources.md) derives their specialization to unital channels and the resulting endpoint-time budget. Its min-entropy conclusion and finite-controller inequality have their explicitly stated resource models; they do not create an unconditional thermodynamic-cost theorem.

## The complete relationship

The listed works establish the memory mechanism, bottleneck reasoning, local preparation, intermediate-reservoir acceleration, classical renewal intuition, and general channel tools. The combined conclusion here is a fixed-Hamiltonian comparison: an exponential local obstruction for every unital endpoint protocol, a universal one-switch polynomial erasure construction, an exact fixed-filling coherent-channel exponent, and a necessary-and-sufficient filling scale for bounded spin-independent reservoir schedules. The proofs identify where the non-Gaussian physical input, number coherences, and reference systems enter that conclusion.
